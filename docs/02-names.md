# Names

The game is called **Latchsoil**.

A survey latch was the thing that held the ring's hunger shut. The soil is what the player actually has. The word is invented for this game.

The world and the title are separate. The proper nouns below stay if the title ever changes.

## Peoples

Harth, Cindervault, Brinelung, Marrowquiet, Kiteborn, Thornlattice, Rust Cantors, Sable Choir, Glass Shepherds, Lumenbrood, Keelwights, Mire Saints.

Singular and plural match the name, except Rust Cantors, Glass Shepherds, and Mire Saints, which are already plural. One Rust Cantor. One Glass Shepherd. One Mire Saint. One of the Lumenbrood. One Keelwight. The Harth, when speaking of the people as a whole.

## Play calendar

Lore year is 428 days. The play year is 40 days, 10 days a season, so a run can reach a consequence. Day length defaults to 12 real minutes. Space pauses. Speed controls come with pause.

Do not rename the seasons to spring, summer, autumn, and winter in player-facing text. Those words can appear in design notes when a comparison helps.
