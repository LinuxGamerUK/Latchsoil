# Vision

Latchsoil is a colony sim you watch from above. WASD slides a stylized 3D isometric camera across one seeded world. The people under the camera do the walking, the bleeding, and the building. You select them and tell them what the day is for.

## The play promise

- World, in the spirit of RimWorld: a new seeded landscape every run, with incidents that arrive because the map and the neighbors are alive
- Building and economy, in the spirit of Banished: short production chains, houses, stores, farms, ditches, and seasons that can starve a careless holding
- Bodies, in the spirit of Project Zomboid: hunger, thirst, wounds, infection, and skills that rise because a person did the work
- Other powers, in the spirit of Stellaris: treaties, wars, and ethics, practiced between landed peoples on a single world
- Hands: a colony camera. Drafting and priorities are how work and war happen

The goal of a run is simple. Build the holding. Survive. Thrive.

## What you are in the fiction

You are the charter, not a body on the field. The first names are your starting people, and the founder can die like anyone else. The run continues. A dead founder still hurts: Keelwights lose morale when their ranking officer dies, and any holding that published a law in a founder's name feels the loss.

## What a good moment looks like

It is day 2 of Sprout. The well is a hole and a promise. One Harth is hauling wood, one is eating the last forage meal, and a Glass Shepherd camp has appeared on the salt pan with three shardbeasts and a price. The ditch is still inside the peg's acre. Pushing it further is a choice the neighbors will remember.

## Pillars

1. The sim is the game. A headless test can starve a holding, save it, and load it back.
2. Laws are objects. You publish them on a law-loom. They cost material and reputation. They change how your people act and how neighbors judge you.
3. Money is fuel. Glass chits are cut from the fallen ring. Smelters drink them. Spending cash melts the sky. Hoarding cash hoards power.
4. Territory is dirt and paper. The survey peg stays. The ditch around it is the border. Moving the ditch past the granted acre without a new paper is how a farm becomes a war.
5. Good and evil are records of acts, not a meter you spend. A holding can keep its word and still have an open appetite.
6. Every people reads at a glance. Silhouette, material, and accent color identify them at holding zoom.

## Out of scope until a later charter

Other planets, flight, multiplayer, voice acting, a scripted campaign, player mods beyond editing data files, vehicles, a cover system, and twelve fully unique animation sets. The design target is still a 4 km map and all twelve peoples. The milestones reach that through a 512 m Harth loop first.

## Tone

Weathered and plain-spoken. The sky is beautiful and wrong. Cruel acts are available and remembered. The writing states the record, the law, and the consequence. It does not gloat, and it does not show gore as spectacle. Injury reads as a decal, a limp, and a clock.
