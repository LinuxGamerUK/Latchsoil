# Peoples

Twelve peoples. Baseline for every stat is 50. Numbers below are biases, not caps. The player-default people are the **Harth**.

Global body rule: stylized mid-poly, one silhouette and one material language per people. Full shared art rules are in [08-art.md](08-art.md).

Stats used here: Heat, Cold, Thirst, Hunger tolerance, Craft, Farm, Fight, Talk, Learn, Stealth, Machine, Scout, Carry, Health, speed. An unlisted stat is 50.

## 1. Harth

The charter line that refused further edits after the Shedding. Weathered human faces, sun creases, canvas over ceramic, one inherited scrap of ring-cloth blue that nobody can dye anymore.

- Accent: that uncopyable blue, used rarely
- Art: timber frames, ceramic shingles ground from glass, soot and ochre. Straw hats and salvage visors. Painterly textures, stocky bodies.
- Body default: 1.7 m, working build, warm skin, layered work clothes, visor optional
- Stats: 50 across. The teaching people.
- Needs: standard hunger, thirst, cold, and heat
- Ethic lean: kept word
- Home: temperate river valleys
- Kit: hatchet, hoe, waterskin, 20 grain, canvas tent, one blue scrap with no combat value
- Fantasy: the ditch, the peg, ordinary courage
- Voice: "The peg stays. The ditch moves when the paper says."

## 2. Cindervault

Born in glass wastes that stayed hot for decades. Ash-grey skin, copper freckles that brighten with fever or anger, deep-set eyes. They fuse. They rarely nail.

- Accent: ember orange
- Art: obsidian, copper, kiln-arches, charcoal. Cloaks like cooled folds of slag. A rim light on the silhouette.
- Body default: 1.75 m, dense build, ash skin, copper freckles, respirator at the throat
- Stats: Heat 80, Cold 25, Craft 70, Farm 30, Fight 60, Talk 35
- Needs: Deep Bell hurts. Staying warm anywhere else costs extra fuel.
- Ethic lean: open appetite
- Home: glass basins and geothermal shelves
- Kit: fusing rod, slag hammer, respirator, obsidian knife, no seed
- Fantasy: make wealth, then buy or take food
- Voice: "Cold food is a confession."

## 3. Brinelung

Equatorial canal folk, edited for tide farms. Ribbed gill-slits that work in brine and clog in dust, webbed fingers, hair like kelp-wire. They sell salt, oil, and news.

- Accent: brass
- Art: reed, waxed sailcloth, arched bridges, boats that are houses. Teal, brass, bone white. Wide shoulders, trailing coats, lanterns on poles.
- Body default: 1.65 m, broad shoulder, wet-green hair, brass jewelry, webbed hands
- Stats: Thirst 85, Heat 60, Cold 30, Talk 75, Farm 65, Fight 40
- Needs: brackish water is fine. Dry interior maps drain them.
- Ethic lean: kept word, practiced as trade
- Home: coasts, deltas, canals
- Kit: coracle, net, brass cup, tide-rice, short spear
- Fantasy: the trade route is a weapon
- Voice: "The tide kept the books before you did."

## 4. Marrowquiet

Went underground when the sky fell and stayed there. Tall, pale, grey vascular maps under the skin, wide low-light eyes, voices trained down to a whisper. Bone is their index, not their ghost.

- Accent: mushroom amber
- Art: ribbed halls, mycelium plaster, paper lanterns, carved catalog tags. Bone, ink, amber. Long coats, no bright metal.
- Body default: 1.95 m, narrow, paper-pale, ink stationer's cuffs
- Stats: Cold 70, Heat 35, Learn 80, Fight 35, Stealth 70, Farm 60 (fungus)
- Needs: glare and high heat exhaust them. They stay calm on small rations underground.
- Ethic lean: neutral, archival
- Home: karst, caves, sinkholes
- Kit: lamp, bone stylus, spore brick, quiet knife, archive shard
- Fantasy: know the ruin before you open it
- Voice: "If it is not indexed, it did not happen. Yet."

## 5. Kiteborn

Mesa watches, edited for thin air. Light bones, a fold-membrane from wrist to rib for gliding, wind-burned faces, cable braids.

- Accent: one red string
- Art: tensile tents, cliff pegs, sail frames, banners like laundry. Sand and sky-bleach. Narrow on the ground, wide in the air.
- Body default: 1.8 m, very light, membrane cloak, red string in the hair
- Stats: Scout 80, Carry 30, Fight 45, Farm 35, Cold 55
- Needs: cold mesas are fine. Overloading them causes injury. They cannot haul a base on their backs.
- Ethic lean: neutral
- Home: mesas, canyons, wind gaps
- Kit: cable harness, wind knife, dried meat, signal mirror
- Fantasy: see the raid first
- Voice: "The wind posted this morning. You may read it."

## 6. Thornlattice

A syndicate that took a photosynthetic graft and kept their names. Bark plates on scalp and shoulders, a green flush in sunlight, slow feet, seasonal mood. They bleed, cook, and argue.

- Accent: cream blossom
- Art: rooms trained out of hedges, grafted timber, seed mosaics. Sap green, cream, wet bark. Broad leaf-hats over bark crowns. Curves, almost no naked metal.
- Body default: 1.7 m, heavy, bark at the crown and shoulders, flower-cloth
- Stats: Farm 85, Hunger tolerance 70, Cold 20, Fight 40, speed low, fire vulnerability high
- Needs: sun covers part of hunger. They still eat. Winter and fire are the killers.
- Ethic lean: kept word, until starvation
- Home: wet temperate forest and hedge country
- Kit: graft shears, seed belt, water bladder, thorn spear, one living cutting that must be planted within 3 days
- Fantasy: the map itself becomes the wall
- Voice: "Cut me a door, not a wound."

## 7. Rust Cantors

A yard people. Joining means a graft and a canticle, and most are born to it now. Jaw buzzers, tendon winches, lamp-eyes. They sing work-rhythms so old servitors do not panic.

- Accent: one cyan servo light, plus liturgical gold
- Art: cathedral scrap, crane-arm buttresses, instrument glass as windows. Rust, oil black, gold. Asymmetric bodies. Warning stickers worn as icons.
- Body default: 1.75 m, one obvious graft, oil-black coat, gold cord
- Stats: Machine 90, Craft 75, infection resist 70, Fight 55, Farm 25, Talk 30, Hunger harsh
- Needs: high metabolism. Power cells matter as much as bread.
- Ethic lean: neither track by doctrine. A canticle can order them to pull an implant from a corpse or to walk a child out of a fire.
- Home: junkyards and crashed maintenance yards
- Kit: canticle fork, scrap blade, one power cell, one servitor that might boot
- Fantasy: wake the yard
- Voice: "Hold the note. The door is listening."

## 8. Sable Choir

Nocturnal ridge people. Keratin crests like folded ink. Their harmonies carry in stone and hurt untrained ears. This is sound, not psychic power. Guest-right is sacred. Outsiders are a pantry until they become guests.

- Accent: silver
- Art: matte lacquer, silver inlay, tents like closed flowers by day and acoustic shells by night. Sable, silver, dried-blood burgundy. Edge light only.
- Body default: 1.7 m, slight, tall crest, lacquer mask, silver wire
- Stats: Stealth 80, Fight 70, Talk 65 inside the choir, Farm 30, Cold 60, Health 40
- Needs: fragile. Bright camps upset them. Night is when they work.
- Ethic lean: open appetite, with a hard guest-law
- Home: canyons and night ridges
- Kit: choir spike, lacquer mask, dried blood-plum, no lamp
- Fantasy: the night raid and the night market are the same door
- Voice: "You are fed, or you are a guest. Say which."

## 9. Glass Shepherds

Nomads who herd shardbeasts: slow mineral grazers that sweat a glaze-milk used as sealant, pottery, and rough money. Mirror-dust veils, pale gold irises. Settlement is a lapse.

- Accent: pale gold
- Art: low tensile camps, mirror-cloth, beasts included in the silhouette. Mirror white, pale gold, dust pink. Temporary on purpose.
- Body default: 1.7 m, desert-lean, veil, gold eyes, goad at the hip
- Stats: Herding 80, Talk 70, Heat 75, Craft (glaze) 65, Fight 45
- Needs: heat is kind. Ordinary thirst. Buildings cost extra upkeep unless marked as camp.
- Ethic lean: neutral traders
- Home: salt pans and glass plains
- Kit: 3 shardbeasts, veil, goad, glaze jar, no walls
- Fantasy: wealth that walks
- Voice: "We will be east of you by Stitchfall. Pay before then."

## 10. Lumenbrood

A clonal line from a medical hull in the mountains. One face, many names, scars and red thread chosen on purpose. A plague that learns one of them is studying all of them.

- Accent: thread red
- Art: surgical white tile gone dirty, oxidized green, red thread. Too-clean arches in scree. Near-identical faces with one bold personal mark so a group reads at holding zoom.
- Body default: 1.7 m, the shared face, one red-thread pattern, a clinical coat over travel dirt
- Stats: Learn 75, Medicine 80, Fight 45, Farm 50, Talk 60, plague vulnerability 80
- Needs: disease clocks run hotter. Medicine skill starts high.
- Ethic lean: unaligned. Play splits the line into mercy or cold triage.
- Home: mountain scree beside a cracked white hull
- Kit: medkit, thread needle, sterile tarp, scalpel, 4 colonists who share a face
- Fantasy: identity as a craft, medicine as a risk
- Voice: "That one is me. The scar is the difference you asked for."

## 11. Keelwights

Crew who never left the landed ships. Rank is a birthright. They farm badly in what they still call unsealed dirt, and they fight in corridors even when the corridor is a field.

- Accent: warning yellow on cobalt
- Art: naval brutalism, bulkhead doors, cobalt paint over rust, hazard stripes. Uniform coats. Rank is the braid. Interiors carry the fantasy.
- Body default: 1.75 m, straight posture, cobalt coat, braid, pressure scars at the collar
- Stats: Fight 70, Machine 60, Farm 30, Cold 65, Heat 45
- Needs: morale cracks if the ranking officer dies. Food production is poor.
- Ethic lean: lawful. Kindness is optional, and it is ordered.
- Home: dead hulls on coasts or in craters
- Kit: boarding axe, pressure coat, 3 ranked crew, sealed rations, one working door
- Fantasy: a ship that became a town
- Voice: "Dirt is a compartment with the lid lost. Farm it anyway."

## 12. Mire Saints

Voluntary fungal symbiosis, practiced as care. Bioluminescent patches, a sweet smell that means salve or spore-sickness according to discipline. Quarantine and guest-right are the same law.

- Accent: spore gold
- Art: cathedral mushrooms, damp timber, gauze banners, low fog. Violet, spore gold, wet brown. Soft light from the skin.
- Body default: 1.65 m, soft glow at the throat and wrists, gauze layers, no long blade
- Stats: Medicine 85, Farm 70 (fungus), Talk 60, Fight 30, Cold 40
- Needs: neighbors fear their care. Their own infection track is managed, not absent.
- Ethic lean: kept word by doctrine. Weaponized bloom is the heresy on the appetite track.
- Home: marshes and fogged river bends
- Kit: gauze, spore lamp, broth, harvest knife
- Fantasy: the healer a village might drive off
- Voice: "I can close that. You may refuse my hands."

## Data rule

When these peoples become files, one RON file per people lives in `data/races/`. The file holds the numbers, the kit, the home biome, the ethic lean, the opening relations, and the voice line. A new people must not require a new branch in the simulation.
